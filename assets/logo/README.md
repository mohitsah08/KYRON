## Usage Guidelines

1. **Proportions & Geometry**: Maintain the exact isometric interlocking "K" aspect ratio (1024:959). Do not stretch, skew, or rotate the emblem.
2. **Clear Space**: Ensure adequate breathing room around the mark equal to at least 20% of the emblem's width.
3. **No Redrawing or Modification**: Do not substitute or approximate the logo.
